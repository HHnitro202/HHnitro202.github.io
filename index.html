How I work

Good engineering is often less about shiny frameworks and more about knowing what not to break.

Understand the system first

Trace the current behavior, identify the real boundaries, and avoid breaking auth/login or other critical paths.

Make the core behavior correct

Get the feature working with clear tests and logs before spending time on polish or premature abstraction.

Harden the result

Clean the code, reduce edge-case risk, improve data validation and document the operational assumptions.
